Weekly puzzle 1: The Manor: the vanished heir

Level 2 · Sleuth ★★☆☆☆ · Published November 23, 2026

Print the puzzle (PDF)Free on Google Play

The boudoir smells of perfume and secrets.

Clues

A · Albert (♂ man): Was directly next to a chair.

B · Barbara (♀ woman): Was alone in the boudoir.

C · Charles (♂ man): Was directly next to a plant.

D · Donna (♀ woman): Was in the 4th row.

V · Vaughan (♂ man): the victim. The murderer is the only suspect in the same room.

How to play

one person per row and per column · "next to" = directly above/below/left/right, in the same room · nobody can be on blocked objects (⊘) · the victim has no clue · attributes (♂ man, ♀ woman, glasses, hat) follow each name; when counting "in total", the person themselves and the victim are included.
