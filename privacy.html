English

Cluedoku is a puzzle game by Kelsey van der Heijden. You play without an account.

  • Everything stays on your phone. Your progress, scores, settings, chosen language, credit and purchases are stored only on your own device. We receive none of it. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, that information is gone.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. The app has no account, no statistics and no tracking of its own, and does not send any data to us.
  • Ads from Google AdMob. The app only shows an ad when you ask for one, for example for an extra hint or to open a case. These ads come from Google AdMob. Google processes data such as your IP address, your device's advertising ID and information about your device and the app, among other things to show and measure ads and to prevent abuse. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first; you can change your choice later in the settings under "Privacy choices for ads". More about this: how Google uses data and Google's privacy policy.
  • Purchases through Google Play. When you buy something in the app, Google Play handles the payment. We receive no payment details, name or email address; the app only learns from Google Play which products you have bought.
  • Feedback. If you email us from the app, we use your email address and your message only to reply to you.
  • Children. The app is not intended for children under 13.
  • Changes. If this policy changes, the new date is shown at the top of this page.
